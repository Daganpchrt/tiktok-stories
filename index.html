Vertical video tool

Daily Story

Daily Story turns a short text story into a fully narrated, captioned, vertical video — AI voice narration, word-by-word synced subtitles, and an assembled background — then publishes it straight to TikTok through TikTok's official Content Posting API.

01 How it works

A short piece of text goes in; a ready-to-post vertical video comes out. The whole pipeline runs end to end without manual video editing.

1

Write the story

A short story script is prepared as plain text — in French or English.

2

AI narration

The text is read aloud by a natural-sounding neural voice, at a controllable pace.

3

Synced captions

Speech-to-text timing (word-level) drives karaoke-style captions, highlighting each word as it's spoken.

4

Publish

The narration, captions and background are assembled into one 1080×1920 video and posted to TikTok via the Content Posting API.

03 Features

NARRATION

Neural text-to-speech in French and English, adjustable rate, no recording needed.

CAPTIONS

Word-level timing from automatic speech recognition, burned in with the spoken word highlighted as it plays.

BACKGROUND ASSEMBLY

Background footage is stitched from multiple clips per video so nothing loops visibly during narration.

TWO-PART SPLITTING

Longer stories can be split into two videos at a natural cliffhanger point, for a "Part 1 / Part 2" format.

DIRECT PUBLISHING

Finished videos are posted straight to TikTok through the official Content Posting API — no manual export/upload step.

CAPTION GENERATION

A suggested TikTok caption (with hashtags) is generated alongside each video.

04 Terms of Service

What this is

Daily Story generates narrated vertical videos from text and publishes them to connected TikTok accounts using TikTok's Content Posting API, as described above.

Access

TikTok accounts are connected one at a time, deliberately, by the account holder authorizing access through TikTok's own OAuth consent screen — the tool never acts on an account without that explicit authorization.

Acceptable use

  • The tool is used only to publish content that has been produced and reviewed before posting.
  • All publishing goes through TikTok's official Content Posting API and remains subject to TikTok's own Terms of Service and Content Sharing Guidelines, in addition to this document.
  • The tool does not scrape TikTok, impersonate other accounts, or act on any account without that account's own OAuth authorization.

No warranty

Provided as-is, with no uptime or support commitment.

05 Privacy Policy

This policy covers the TikTok accounts connected to Daily Story. The tool collects nothing from anyone simply browsing this page, and nothing from TikTok viewers of the posted videos.

STORED LOCALLY

OAuth access & refresh tokens, one file per connected TikTok account, on the machine running the tool. Never uploaded anywhere.

SENT TO TIKTOK

Only what the API requires to publish a video: the video file, its caption, and the requested privacy setting.

NEVER COLLECTED

No analytics, no tracking pixels, no viewer or follower data, nothing sold or shared with a third party.

Data TikTok returns to the app

DataPurpose
open_idIdentifies which connected account a token belongs to.
access / refresh tokenLets the tool publish on behalf of the account that granted it, until revoked.
Creator info (privacy options, posting limits)Read immediately before each post, as TikTok's API requires, to submit a valid request.

Retention & deletion

Tokens are kept until the account owner disconnects the app (from TikTok's own account settings) or deletes the local token file. Deleting the file or revoking access in TikTok immediately stops the tool from posting.
